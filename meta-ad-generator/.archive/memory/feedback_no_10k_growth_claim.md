In Sharpify LV warning/targeting ads ("Meklējam 5 X meistarus ar savu firmu, kas..."), do NOT use "kas gatavi augt līdz €10k+ mēnesī ar AI mārketinga sistēmu". Use "kas gatavi uzņemt jaunus klientus katru mēnesi ar AI mārketinga sistēmu" instead.

**Why:** Boss instruction (2026-04-15) — promising €10k+ monthly revenue as a targeting qualifier is too aggressive / claim-heavy for Meta warning ads. Client-acquisition framing is safer and truer to the offer.

**How to apply:** Any new LV warning-style ad template (`-4-warning.html`, `-warning`, "Meklējam X meistarus" framing). After any ad edit that touches this phrase, re-render the PNG via `scripts/export-png.js` (memory: "Delete HTML when deleting PNG"). This rule applies to ad *targeting/qualifier copy* — real past client results (e.g. "Baiba Aucīte 3 gados līdz €10K/mēn" in case-study carousels) are fine because those are factual outcomes, not a promise to new leads.
