**How to apply:** On ENG MS-funnel ads, copy the LV chip behavior: "🎁 Free website included" on arched template, "🎁 Free website included" on sticker card gift element. The "Playbook is not free" rule still applies to the lead magnet PDF itself — never call the downloadable playbook free. But the website bonus IS free when someone buys MS, and that's what the chip references.
