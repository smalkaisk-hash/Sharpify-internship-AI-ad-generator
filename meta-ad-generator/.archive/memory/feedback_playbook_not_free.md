The B2B Client Acquisition Playbook at eu.sharpify.lv/ms/ is NOT free. Never use "free", "free playbook", "free training" etc. in ad copy, headlines, or on-image text.

**Why:** User explicitly corrected this. The landing page may say "free" but the actual product is paid. Calling it free in ads creates wrong expectations and is misleading.

**How to apply:** When creating ads for the ENG playbook funnel, use value-focused CTAs like "Get the Playbook", "Download the Playbook", "Get Instant Access" — without the word "free". Same applies to primary text and headlines.
