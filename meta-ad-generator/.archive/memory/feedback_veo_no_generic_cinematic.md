Don't prompt Veo for generic "cinematic commercial" footage for Meta ads (founder typing, generic website on screen, handshake, walking through cafe). Looks nice, gives the viewer no reason to stop scrolling.

**Why:** On Meta, pretty b-roll = scrolled past. Ads need a pattern interrupt in the first 0.5–1s, a visual hook the viewer can't predict, or a concrete story beat (weird, funny, conflict, before/after with strong payoff, UGC-native phone footage, screen recordings, meme energy). Cinematic polish reads as "ad" and gets skipped.

**How to apply:** When prompting Veo for Sharpify/client Meta ads, lead with the hook concept, not the aesthetic. Ask: "what makes someone stop in the first second?" Prefer UGC/phone feel, visual metaphors, text-on-screen moments, absurd or funny framings, or strong before/after with a clear dollar/client payoff — over moody 35mm dolly shots. Save cinematic polish for YouTube pre-roll or website hero reels, not feed ads.
