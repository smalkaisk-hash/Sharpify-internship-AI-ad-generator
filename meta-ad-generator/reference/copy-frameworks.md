# Ad Copy Frameworks Reference

## Framework 1: PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)

### Structure
1. **Problem**: State the pain point your audience feels
2. **Agitate**: Amplify the emotional weight of that problem
3. **Solution**: Present your offer as the relief

### Template
```
[Problem — 1 sentence, relatable]
[Agitate — 1-2 sentences, emotional amplification]
[Solution — 1-2 sentences, your offer as the answer]
[CTA — clear next step]
```

### Example (Latvian — coaching)
```
Primary: Vai jūties tā, it kā dzīvo "autopilotā" — ej uz darbu, dari visu pareizi, bet iekšā nav skaidrības?
Tu neesi viena. Daudzas sievietes jūtas tieši tā — nogurušas no šaubām un iekšējās nenoteiktības.
"Iekšējā skaidrība" ir 8 nedēļu koučinga programma, kas palīdz atgūt pārliecību un pieņemt lēmumus bez bailēm.
Headline: Atgūsti skaidrību 8 nedēļās
CTA: Piesakies bezmaksas sarunai
```

## Framework 2: AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)

### Structure
1. **Attention**: Bold hook — question, statistic, or provocative statement
2. **Interest**: Expand with relevant detail that resonates
3. **Desire**: Paint the outcome / transformation
4. **Action**: Clear CTA

### Template
```
[Attention — hook question or bold claim]
[Interest — "here's what that means for you..."]
[Desire — "imagine..." or outcome description]
[Action — specific CTA with urgency if natural]
```

---

## Framework 3: Before → After → Bridge

### Structure
1. **Before**: Describe their current painful state
2. **After**: Describe life after transformation
3. **Bridge**: Your offer is the bridge between the two

### Template
```
[Before — "Right now you feel..."]
[After — "Imagine feeling..."]
[Bridge — "That's exactly what [offer] does. Here's how to start:"]
```

---

## Framework 4: Question Hook

### Structure
1. Open with a provocative question that the target audience will say "yes" to
2. Short elaboration
3. Offer + CTA

### Best for: Awareness campaigns, cold audiences

### Template
```
[Question that triggers self-identification]
[1-2 sentences of validation / elaboration]
[Offer + CTA]
```

---

## Framework 5: Testimonial / Social Proof Lead

### Structure
1. Open with a quote or result from a real client
2. Brief context of the transformation
3. CTA to get similar results

### Template
```
"[Client quote or result]"
[Context — who they were before, what changed]
[CTA — "Ready for your transformation?"]
```

## Copy Rules for All Frameworks

### Language & Tone
- Match the client's language (auto-detected from brief)
- Use conversational, warm tone for coaching/services
- Use direct, benefit-focused tone for products/e-commerce
- Avoid jargon unless audience expects it
- Use "you" language — speak directly to the reader

### Hook Writing Rules
- First line must stop the scroll — max 10 words
- Use: questions, bold claims, "imagine" statements, numbers, or controversy
- Front-load the benefit or pain point
- Avoid generic openers ("Are you looking for...", "Do you want...")

### CTA Writing Rules
- Be specific: "Piesakies bezmaksas sarunai" > "Uzzini vairāk"
- Match the funnel stage: awareness = soft CTA, retargeting = direct CTA
- Include what happens next: "Book a free 30-min call"
- One CTA per ad — never split attention

### Emoji Usage
- 1-3 emojis per primary text, placed strategically
- Use as visual line breaks or bullet points
- Never in headlines
- Common high-performers: ✨ 🔥 💡 ➡️ ✅ 💪 🎯

### Multilingual Notes
- Generate all copy in the client's primary language
- If target audience is bilingual, consider one variation in each language
- Cultural tone: Latvian audiences respond to warmth and authenticity over hype
- Formal/informal: Use informal "tu" for coaching/personal brands, formal "Jūs" for corporate
