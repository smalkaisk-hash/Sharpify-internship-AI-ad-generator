# Meta Ad Specifications Reference

## Feed Ad (Primary Format)

### Dimensions
- **Size**: 1080 x 1080 px (1:1 square)
- **Min resolution**: 600 x 600 px
- **Max file size**: 30 MB (image), 4 GB (video)
- **Format**: PNG or JPG (PNG preferred for text-heavy ads)

### Safe Zones
- **Top 150px**: Avoid critical content (profile name overlay)
- **Bottom 120px**: Avoid critical content (CTA button overlay area)
- **Side margins**: Keep 40px padding minimum on all sides
- **Center safe zone**: 900 x 810 px (main content area)

### Text Overlay Guidelines
- Meta no longer enforces a strict 20% text rule, but **ads with less text perform better**
- Recommended: keep text overlay under 20% of image area
- Always ensure text contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 against background (WCAG AA)
- Use text shadows or overlay gradients to ensure readability over photos

## Ad Copy Limits

### Primary Text (above the image)
- **Recommended**: 125 characters (shows without "See more")
- **Maximum**: 2,200 characters
- First 125 chars are critical — front-load the hook
- Emojis allowed and perform well for engagement

### Headline (below the image)
- **Recommended**: 27 characters
- **Maximum**: 255 characters
- Appears bold below the image, next to CTA button
- Should be a clear value proposition or action prompt

### Description (below headline)
- **Recommended**: 27 characters
- **Maximum**: 255 characters
- Not always shown — depends on placement and screen size
- Use as supporting detail, never critical info

## Image Best Practices
- Use high-resolution source images (min 1080px on shortest side)
- Avoid heavy filters — Meta compresses images further
- Human faces in ads increase engagement by 30-40%
- Product/service images outperform stock photos
- Brand colors should be visible within first 0.5 seconds of viewing
- Logo placement: top-left or bottom-right corner, sized 80-120px

## Video Specs (for future use)
- **Aspect ratio**: 1:1 (feed), 9:16 (stories/reels)
- **Duration**: 1s to 241 minutes (recommended: 15-30s)
- **Format**: MP4, MOV
- **Resolution**: 1080 x 1080 minimum
- **Captions**: Required for accessibility, improve performance by 12%
