# Template 47: Bold Headline Review Card

Use the attached images as brand reference. Create: a 1:1 static ad on a [BACKGROUND COLOR like soft green] background. Top 25%: "[HEADLINE like Say No To Bloating]" in very large, bold, black sans-serif text — this must be the dominant visual element. Center: a white rounded-rectangle review card with subtle shadow. Inside the card: [STAR COUNT] gold/yellow star icons centered at top. Below: "[REVIEW TEXT]" in regular weight dark text, justified or centered. Below the review: "— [REVIEWER NAME]" in bold italic, with "[REVIEWER BADGE like (Verified Reviewer)]" in lighter weight beside it. Below the card: product image angled slightly, overlapping the bottom of the card edge. Bottom-left: [BRAND LOGO] small. The headline-to-review flow should feel like: bold claim → proof → product. 1:1 aspect ratio.
