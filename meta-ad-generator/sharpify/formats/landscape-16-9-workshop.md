# Landscape 16:9 Workshop Ad

**Status:** Proven winner. €134 spend → 18 purchases at €7.44 CPA — account top-performer for workshop/digital-product campaigns (vs €9-14 CPA range for 1:1 formats).

**When to use:** AI Rīku Komplekts, workshop campaigns, direct-purchase digital product campaigns where 16:9 landscape placement is available. Always offer this variant alongside 1:1 for these campaigns.

## Dimensions

1280×720 (16:9 landscape)

## Visual spec — Sharpify landscape design language

- **Background:** dark gradient `linear-gradient(145deg, #0a0a14, #0f1020, #0a0a12)` with subtle gold + green radial glows
- **Gold italic accents:** Playfair Display serif `font-style: italic` + gold gradient `#f5d76e → #daa520 → #b8860b`
- **Headlines:** Montserrat 900
- **Body:** Inter regular/medium
- **Wordmark:** "Sharpify" always bottom-right in Playfair italic gold
- **Icons:** Lucide/Feather style inline SVG (`stroke="#10b981"` or `#daa520`, `fill="none"`, `stroke-width="1.75"`) — NO emoji

## Copy structure

- **Title:** `"Izveidot Savu [X] | Rezervē Biļeti"`
- **Body opening:** `"[N] stundās Tu uzbūvēsi..."`
- **4-line benefit block:** each line prefixed with `— [benefit]`
- **Specific date:** when the workshop/offer is live
- **Pricing:** `"Sākot no €X"`
- **Guarantee:** `"100% naudas atgriešanas garantija."`

## Key messaging principle

**Time-investment framing outperforms feature lists.**

- Good: "2 stundas → 10h+ nedēļā"
- Good: "3 stundās iemācies izmantot Claude Code visam biznesam"
- Bad: bulleted feature list without the time trade articulation

The viewer wants to know what hours of THEIR life are worth what hours of payoff.
