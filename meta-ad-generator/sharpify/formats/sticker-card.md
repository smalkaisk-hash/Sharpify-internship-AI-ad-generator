# Sticker Card Template

**Status:** Approved second format alongside arched-saas. Used across niches.

**When to use:** Alternative to arched-saas when photo is strong and you want the copy + proof element (notification) to feel "stuck on" the image.

## Visual spec

- **Background:** full-bleed photo (documentary persona or niche context)
- **Card:** dark card at bottom, rotated -1.4°, 6px accent top border
- **Card content:** headline + short body + CTA
- **Notification:** white iPhone-style card top-right, rotated +2.4°, shows niche-specific fake "Jauns pieteikums" (see `notification-card.md`)

## Why it works

The notification element turns abstract "AI system" into tangible proof of lead flow. The slight rotations break from the rigid-grid feel without being distracting. User approved across niches.

## Typography

- Card headline: 46px (three lines → 36px)
- Card body: smaller supporting text
- Notification timestamp + content uses smaller casual sizes

## Languages

Works in both LV and ENG. Pair with:
- LV: "Pieteikumi nāk paši, kamēr Tu [activity]" headline (doesn't have the "parūpējies" issue — stays literal)
- ENG: equivalent "leads arrive while you [activity]" framing

## Accent colors by niche category

- Construction / home improvement / general trades → amber `#F59E0B`
- Auto service → red `#DC2626`
- Beauty / wellness → warm gold `#C9A878` on dark brown `#1a1410` / `#14100d` bg
