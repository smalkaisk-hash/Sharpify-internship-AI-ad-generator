## Copy & Tone

### 2026-04-14 — Primary text formula for ENG Sharpify ads
**Rule:** Opens with identity question ("Are you a business owner who..."), followed by 4 ✅ pain points, transition ("Here's what we do differently"), 4-5 🔹 features, social proof line, 👉 CTA, signed "Niks Jansons, CEO @ Sharpify". Empathetic and long-form, not descriptive.
**Why:** User showed me the exact "Are you a coach..." template and said "take this style." Short descriptive copy was rejected.

### 2026-04-14 — Headlines must be short identity hooks, NOT ad descriptions
**Rule:** Write headlines that make the reader think "that's me" — e.g. "Still searching for the right website?" — NOT descriptions of the ad creative ("Still searching? Your competitors already found this."). Keep under ~40 chars so they don't overflow in Meta's preview.
**Why:** User: "the headlines don't fit in the ad, they go off... we need something that will make them think like yeah, that's me, I should buy."

### 2026-04-14 — Never claim unverified stars or ratings
**Rule:** Don't include "★★★★★", "5-star", or "1,000+ five-star reviews" in ad creatives unless verified from Trustpilot/Google. "2,300+ businesses served" is fine (verifiable via press).
**Why:** User: "I'm not really sure if we actually have 5 stars or not" — told me to strip them from ad-7 (Google) and ad-10 (ChatGPT).

## Visual & Design

### 2026-04-14 — Cross-out comparisons: cross out only the price, not the whole row
**Rule:** When showing competitor prices being "beaten" by Sharpify, draw the strike-through line ONLY across the price number. Don't strike through the entire row/item.
**Why:** User: "we don't need to cross out the whole page, we can just cross out the price."

### 2026-04-14 — No brand footer strip at the bottom of every ad
**Rule:** Don't add a persistent "Sharpify · web.sharpify.lv" footer bar at the bottom of ad creatives. Keep brand presence to inline price treatments or CTA buttons.
**Why:** User: "for the ads that have footers, with sharpify and the site, lets fully get rid of it."

### 2026-04-14 — CTA buttons: bigger, text must fit, drop the arrow when crowded
**Rule:** CTA button text must fit cleanly inside the button (no ellipsis, no line breaks). If "Get Yours →" crowds the box, drop the arrow and keep "Get Yours". Scale button padding generously for mobile.
**Why:** User: "for ad-13 in the button lets get rid of the arrow, lets just keep Get Yours and lets make the text fit the box."

### 2026-04-14 — Scale text bigger for mobile viewers
**Rule:** Headlines 60-74px, body 28-36px, stats 42-64px, price slam 54-88px. Default mockups render too small on phone feed.
**Why:** User: "we need to make the text a little bit bigger, for our clients that are gonna be watching these on their phone."

## Niches / Product

### 2026-04-14 — ENG Website Offer product is different from B2B Playbook
**Rule:** The ENG account runs two separate offers: (a) **B2B Playbook** at eu.sharpify.lv/ms/ — lead-gen for consulting/coaches, long-form "MS Solution" framing, €50M+ generated claim; (b) **Website Offer** at web.sharpify.lv — direct purchase for service providers needing a website, €59-199, "built in 1-3 days." Don't mix copy from the Playbook skill template into website ads. Website buyers are often newer/scrappier — solo founders, trades, local service businesses — not the same audience as the Playbook.
**Why:** The default ENG skill template (B2B Playbook / MS Solution) doesn't fit the €59 website offer's audience or buying behavior.
