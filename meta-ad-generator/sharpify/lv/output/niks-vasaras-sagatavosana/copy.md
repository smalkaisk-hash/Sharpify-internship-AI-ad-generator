# niks-vasaras-sagatavosana — Ad Copy

Primary text v1 (checkmark "Salona īpašniece" winner formula) — lietojam ar F1 Arched un F3 Hazard.

Headlines — 4 unikālas uz nišu (viena uz katru formātu).
