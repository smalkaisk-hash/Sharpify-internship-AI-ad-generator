I need to be honest about something.

I ran my consulting business for 14 months without a website.

I told myself I didn't need one. "Referrals are enough." "I'll get to it eventually." "It's too expensive."

Meanwhile, potential clients were Googling my name and finding nothing.

Last week I finally got one. Took 2 days. Cost me €59.

This week? 4 new enquiries. All from people who said "I checked out your website first."

14 months of lost opportunities because I kept putting it off.

If you're a service business without a website — stop waiting. It's not 2005 anymore. You don't need to spend thousands. You don't need to learn code. You just need to do it.
