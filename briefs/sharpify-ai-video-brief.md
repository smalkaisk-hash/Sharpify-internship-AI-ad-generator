## SECTION 4 — AD STRUCTURE TEMPLATE

Every Sharpify ad follows this 4-act structure (~13-15 seconds total):

| Scene | Duration | Purpose | Emotional beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. HOOK** | 0-3s | Pattern interrupt — stop scroll | Curiosity / shock |
| **2. PROBLEM or DEMO** | 3-8s | Show pain or new way | Recognition |
| **3. PROOF / DETAILS** | 8-12s | Specifics, features, demo | Credibility |
| **4. PRICE + CTA** | 12-15s | Slash old price, slam new, CTA | Action |

### Standard transitions to use (in order of preference):
1. **Hard cut + brief flash** — punchy, modern (preferred)
2. **Mask wipe (clip-path)** — sweeping reveal from one side, edge has glow line
3. **Iris/circle reveal** — expanding circle reveals new scene
4. **Cross-dissolve** — only for emotional/cinematic moments

### Standard "wow" moments to include:
- **Price slam:** Old price `€99` with red diagonal line drawing across it, then new price `€19` punches in from 8x scale with motion blur + screen shake + brief bass sound
- **Number counter:** Big number races up from 0 to target value (use `tabular-nums`)
- **Item slide-in:** Cards/items slide in from edge with motion blur + 3D perspective tilt
- **Typewriter command:** Terminal-style `/command-name` typed character by character with blinking cursor

## SECTION 6 — MUSIC & AUDIO DIRECTION

**Genre suggestions** (in order of preference):
1. **Cinematic tech / dark electronic** — building bassline, subtle synths, drops on key moments
2. **Modern hip-hop instrumental** — punchy 808s, snappy snares, works for energetic ads
3. **Ambient / atmospheric** — for emotional/cinematic ads (Rihards client style)

**Pacing rules:**
- BPM: 110-130 for energetic ads, 80-100 for cinematic
- Beat drops should align with: scene transitions, price reveal, CTA appearance
- Sound effects: whoosh on slides, ding on completions, bass impact on price slam, brief white-noise riser before drops

**Voiceover:**
- Generally **NO voiceover** — text-driven ads perform better in feed (most people scroll with sound off)
- If using VO: deep confident male voice, Latvian native, no over-pronunciation, conversational pace
