# Documentary Photo Leadgen (LV)

**Status:** The proven LV winner. "Salona īpašniece" ad hit €5.21/lead at 6.77% CTR — account's best-ever leadgen performer.

**When to use:** First-choice format for any LV service-provider leadgen campaign (roofer, IT, construction, beauty, etc.). Beats designed creatives across every test.

## Visual spec

**Zero design elements.** The creative is a single authentic-looking documentary-style photo of the target persona in their work environment.

- NO text overlay
- NO graphics or icons
- NO CTA button on the image
- NO logo
- NO badges

All copy lives in Meta's primary text field.

## Photo prompt formula (Imagen 4)

```
Documentary-style candid photograph of a professional [occupation] in his/her 40s
wearing [work-appropriate outfit]. Confident relaxed posture, slight smile, looking at camera.
Overcast natural daylight, slightly desaturated muted colors. Shot on 35mm lens, shallow depth of field.
Authentic Latvian [niche] entrepreneur portrait, unposed. No text, no logos, no AI-looking artifacts.
```

Tested successfully on jumiķi, IT, būvnieki — all came out realistic.

## Copy structure (9 parts)

Primary text below the image uses this exact structure:

1. **Hook:** `"Meklēju vēl pāris [niche] profesionāļus, kas labprāt uzņemtu vairāk klientu."`
2. **Qualification:** `"Vislabāk varam palīdzēt [niche]iem, kas:"` + 4 ✅ criteria
3. **Industry context:** how clients typically come in that niche (word-of-mouth, referrals)
4. **Identity challenge:** `"Bet ko darīt, ja [pain point]?"`
5. **Pain amplification:** paragraph expanding the problem
6. **Solution intro:** `"Tādā gadījumā Tev ir nepieciešams ātrs un paredzams veids, kā piesaistīt..."`
7. **Credibility:** `"pēdējo gadu laikā esam sadarbojušies ar desmitiem..."`
8. **Soft CTA:** `"atbildi uz pāris jautājumiem anketā zemāk"`
9. **Signature:** `"Stay sharp / Niks"`

## Why it beats designed creatives

Authenticity. The viewer reads "this is a real person, not an ad." Scrolling brain treats it as organic content until they're already engaged with the copy. Designed ads trigger ad-avoidance instantly.

## Performance proof

- "Salona īpašniece" (salon owner): €5.21/lead, 6.77% CTR
- Applied to jumiķi, IT, būvnieki — all show strong early CTR (>3%)
