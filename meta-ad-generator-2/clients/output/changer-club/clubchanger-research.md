## Notes & Observations

- **"Chriss Voss"** is spelled with a double-s on the actual website — likely a typo on their part
- The page has **no traditional navigation menu** — it is a single long-scroll landing page
- The footer **repeats twice** (appears to be a duplicate render in the HTML)
- No copyright year line is present in the footer
- No Terms of Service or Privacy Policy links are visibly linked in the footer
- The brand consistently uses **$5M+ / 20M AED** as the dual-currency threshold
- **"UHNWI"** (Ultra High Net Worth Individual) is used in the media section — their target audience label
- Sadia Khan is titled *"Billionaires Relationship Coach"* in the banner announcement
