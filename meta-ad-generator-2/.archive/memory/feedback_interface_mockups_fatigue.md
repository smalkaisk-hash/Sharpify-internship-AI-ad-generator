Interface-mockup ad formats ("pretend it's a Google search," "fake ChatGPT response," "iMessage screenshot," "iPhone Notes disguise," etc.) work as scroll-stoppers the FIRST time but fatigue fast across a scaled campaign.

**Why:** Confirmed across both LV and ENG workflows:
- ENG: User said "these types of ads don't work... you said you had a solution, but made the same thing." Full diagnosis: no proof of product, no face, format played out, price buried, wrong for visual product.
- LV AI Workshop: User rejected ad-1/2/3/4/5/7/8/9/10/12/17 — all interface/card-based — and approved ad-6/11/13/14/15/16/18 — all editorial/bold typography.

**How to apply:** Mix with editorial, portrait, product-forward, and documentary formats. Use interface mockups at most 1-2 ads per set of 10+. Default to editorial/poster/documentary. Never propose a full campaign built on interface mockups.
