Two-emoji system in Meta ad primary text:
- ✅ checkmarks for **pain point checklists** ("Vislabāk varam palīdzēt tiem, kas:")
- 🔹 blue diamonds for **feature/benefit lists** ("Ar MP Risinājums™ ieviešam:")

DO NOT use any other decorative emojis (🔧, 🤔, 🏆, ⭐, 🌍, 📰, 👇 etc.) — business owners don't want to see decorative emojis in B2B ads.

**Why:** User corrected this multiple times. First push had no emojis (bad). Second push had too many decorative emojis (bad). Third correction established the ✅/🔹 two-emoji system matching their actual ad copy templates.

**How to apply:** ✅ for "problems you face" lists. 🔹 for "what you get" feature lists. Write social proof stats as plain text. No emoji openers, no emoji CTAs, no emoji decorations.
