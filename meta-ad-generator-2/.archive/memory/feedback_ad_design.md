---
name: Ad design preferences
description: User's corrections and preferences for Meta ad generation — no images, no logos, centered text, comparison v4, no repeated info
type: feedback
---

No background images in ads — use solid color gradients with brand colors instead. Clean design always.

**Why:** User tested with real product screenshots and team photos — they looked unprofessional and cluttered in the ad format.

**How to apply:** All 5 ad layouts should use brand color gradients or solid backgrounds. Never add hero photos, product screenshots, or background images unless explicitly requested.

---

No logos on V2 (bold-statement) and V5 (benefit-stack). Templates already updated.

**Why:** Logos clutter these layouts and aren't needed when brand is communicated through colors/text.

**How to apply:** Skip logo placement in these two layouts. Other layouts: only if client requests.

---

Center all text by default. Break long headlines into organized lines with `<br>` tags.

**Why:** Left-aligned text on solid backgrounds looks unbalanced. Long single-line headlines look unprofessional.

**How to apply:** Add `text-align: center; align-items: center;` to content containers. Break headlines so each line is a complete thought.

---

Never repeat the same information twice on one ad (e.g., price in badge AND in CTA button).

**Why:** Looks redundant and unprofessional. Use the second spot for different info like trust signals.

**How to apply:** If price is in CTA, use badge for trust signal or event date instead.

---

V4 should be a Before vs After comparison, NOT an ironic testimonial. Never use made-up reviews.

**Why:** User didn't like the ironic review concept. Fake reviews are a "big nono". Comparison format is more effective and honest.

**How to apply:** V4 layout = two columns, left red (pain points), right green (benefits), 5 items each with emoji icons.
