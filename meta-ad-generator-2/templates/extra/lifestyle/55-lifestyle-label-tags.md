# Template 55: Lifestyle Label Tags

Use the attached images as brand reference. Create: a 1:1 static ad split vertically into two equal lifestyle photos — both showing the product in aspirational real-life settings (e.g., two people wearing suits, two angles of someone in a premium outfit). The photos should be high-quality, editorial-feeling. Overlaid on the photos: 4 floating label tags with [LABEL BACKGROUND COLOR like dark olive/green semi-transparent] backgrounds and white text. Each label is a rounded rectangle positioned at different heights across the two photos: "[LABEL 1]" on the left photo mid-height, "[LABEL 2]" on the right photo upper area, "[LABEL 3]" on the left photo lower area, "[LABEL 4]" on the right photo lower area. The labels should look like they're pinned to the photo, slightly rotated or offset for a natural feel. No headline, no CTA, no logo — the lifestyle imagery + floating labels do all the work. 1:1 aspect ratio.
