# Template 54: Product Grid Discount Panel

Use the attached images as brand reference. Create: a 1:1 static ad divided into a 2x2 grid. Top-left cell: product lifestyle photo (model wearing/using product, clean background). Top-right cell: different product photo or different angle/color. Bottom-left cell: third product variant photo. Bottom-right cell: solid [PANEL BACKGROUND COLOR like black/dark] panel with text stack — "[BRAND NAME]" in small accent-colored text at top, then "[URGENCY HEADLINE like We won't do this again]" in white handwritten/casual serif font, then "[DISCOUNT AMOUNT like 50%]" in enormous bold text with "OFF" smaller beside it, then "[BONUS OFFER like +FREE SHIPPING]" in bold white caps at the bottom. The 3 photo cells should have clean, professional product photography. The discount panel should feel urgent and premium. Grid lines should be minimal/seamless. 1:1 aspect ratio.
