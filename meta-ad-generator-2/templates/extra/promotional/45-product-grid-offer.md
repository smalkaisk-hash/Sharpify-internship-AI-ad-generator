# Template 45: Product Grid Offer Overlay

Use the attached images as brand reference. Create: a 1:1 static ad with a 3x3 grid of product lifestyle photos filling the entire canvas as a background mosaic. Each grid cell shows a different product in a styled setting (flatlay, in-use, or with props). Center of the canvas: a large rounded-rectangle card with [CARD BACKGROUND COLOR like warm cream] background and subtle shadow. Inside the card: "[HEADLINE like Time for a refill?]" in bold dark serif or sans-serif text. Below: "[OFFER TEXT like Subscribe & save 15%!]" in slightly smaller text. Divider line. Below divider: "[CTA TEXT like Save Now!]" in [CTA COLOR like brand blue] bold text acting as a text CTA link. The grid photos should have slight color overlay or reduced brightness so the center card pops. No logo needed — the product photos ARE the branding. 1:1 aspect ratio.
