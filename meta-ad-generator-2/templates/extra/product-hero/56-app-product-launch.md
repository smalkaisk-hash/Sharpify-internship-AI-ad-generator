# Template 56: App/Product Launch Hero

Use the attached images as brand reference. Create: a 1:1 static ad on a solid [BACKGROUND COLOR like dark teal] background. Top center: brand logo in white. Center: "[PRODUCT NAME like Sagatave 3]" in very large bold white text — this is the hero text. Below the product name: app icon or product visual (rounded-corner square icon, or product mockup) centered. If the brand has a mascot: position the mascot character to the right of the icon, slightly overlapping, in a friendly pose (thumbs up, waving). Bottom: "[KEY STAT like 300+ lieliski dizaini]" in large bold white or off-white italic text spanning the width. The overall feel should be: confident product announcement with personality. Clean, bold, minimal — let the product name and stat do the talking. 1:1 aspect ratio.
